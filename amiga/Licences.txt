xu4 for AmigaOS 3.x: what is in the package, and under which terms
====================================================================

The program
-----------
xu4 (https://github.com/xu4-engine/u4, tag pre-alleg, commit
62f3d9096409b5c9a5337d45cb1d3eaa9827276c) is free software under the GNU
General Public License, version 2 or later (COPYING.txt). Its authors are
in upstream's AUTHORS file; the Ultima IV icon used for xu4 is Frank
Wolter's, as AUTHORS says.

The port's own files (amiga/, and the changes marked __amigaos__ in src/)
are by the AmiCradle Team, under the GPL 2 or later like xu4.

Built into the program:
- zlib 1.3.1 (Jean-loup Gailly and Mark Adler): the zlib licence.
- libpng 1.6.44: the PNG Reference Library License version 2.
- libxml2 2.9.14 (Daniel Veillard and others): the MIT licence.
- stb_vorbis 1.22 (Sean Barrett): public domain, or the MIT licence.
- GFOpenGPU's SDL 1.2 (sdl12-compat on SDL 2): the zlib licence.

The data in the drawer
----------------------
conf/, sound/*.ogg and graphics/ (ega, hires, png, vga, vga2, new) are the
files upstream's own 'make install' puts beside xu4, from xu4's GPL
source tree. Upstream gives no licence of their own for the pictures and
sounds beyond the tree's COPYING; some of the pictures are redrawings of
Ultima IV's tiles. graphics/fmtowns (the FM Towns release's pictures) and
upstream's music (mid/: MIDI, MP3 and Impulse Tracker files, which this
port does not play) are not in the package.

Ultima IV itself: NOT in the package
------------------------------------
xu4 needs the PC (DOS) version of Ultima IV: Quest of the Avatar, (c) 1985
Origin Systems, now Electronic Arts. It is given away free (GOG.com has
it as a free download), but it is not free to pass on: Electronic Arts
keeps the copyright and has said that copies may only come from where it
offers them (it had fan sites take their copies down in 2011). So the
port ships none of its files; the player downloads the game and puts its
files in the drawer (see the ReadMe).
